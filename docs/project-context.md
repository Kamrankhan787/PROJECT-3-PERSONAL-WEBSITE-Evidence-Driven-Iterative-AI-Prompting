# Project Context: Kamran Khan Personal Website

## 1. Original Novice Request
The initial user prompt that triggered this project was:
> *"I was in Summer Camp learning AI this June. Now I am thinking to create a personal website that shows everything about me and what I have learned in this Summer Camp. Share what goes into the personal website."*

---

## 2. Why It Produced Generic Results
When presented with the novice prompt, LLMs and generic AI systems produce standard, uninspired portfolio templates due to a complete lack of context:
1. **No Persona Grounding:** The model does not know who the student is, where they studied, or their specific strengths.
2. **Generic "Passionate About Tech" Clichés:** Without specific domains, AI fills gaps with generic statements like *"I am deeply passionate about technology and solving world problems with AI."*
3. **Fictitious / Placeholder Skills:** AI produces bulleted lists of random tools without context of what was actually built.
4. **No Verifiable Artifacts:** No actual code repositories, playable games, syllabus links, or verifiable certificates exist in the prompt.
5. **No Defined Audience:** Without a target audience, the tone waffles between a high school diary and an enterprise consultancy.

---

## 3. Improved Specific Brief
The revised prompt defined explicit positioning, constraints, and architecture:
* **Subject:** Kamran Khan
* **Positioning:** GIAIC Student | Agentic AI & Python Developer | Digital Marketer | Cloud & Applied Generative AI Learner
* **Audience:** Friends, relatives, businesses, potential clients, professional connections, and people interested in AI.
* **Core Questions to Answer:**
  * Who is Kamran Khan?
  * What has he learned?
  * What can he build?
  * What AI tools does he work with?
  * What did he learn from AI Prompting 2026?
  * What projects demonstrate his skills?
  * What certification did he receive?
  * How can someone learn more or contact him?

---

## 4. Personal Information Added
* **Educational Background:** Governor Sindh Initiative for GenAI, Web3 & Metaverse (GIAIC).
* **Specialized Curriculum:** Summer Camp 2026 examination, Panaversity Agent Factory AI Prompting 2026.
* **Competencies:**
  * Agentic AI & Generative AI architectures
  * Python programming & API orchestration

  * Professional AI assistant workflows (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)
  * Cloud computing basics

  * Digital marketing & SEO optimization

---

## 5. Evidence Supplied
Rather than relying on ungrounded text, the website integrates verifiable evidence:
1. **Web Game Development:** References live browser game implementation ([https://snake-game-by-junaid.netlify.app/](https://snake-game-by-junaid.netlify.app/)) showcasing canvas loops and responsive controls without claiming false external ownership.
2. **AI Assistants Workflow:** Demonstrates practical, multi-model prompt design and context engineering across OpenAI ChatGPT, Anthropic Claude, and Google Gemini.
3. **Official Syllabus:** Links directly to [Panaversity AI Prompting 2026 documentation](https://agentfactory.panaversity.org/docs/ai-prompting-2026).
4. **Examination Certificate:** Displays Kamran Khan's Summer Camp 2026 examination achievement with full lightbox modal preview and verified credential details.
5. **Peer & Business Guidance:** Transparent framing of AI guidance services rooted in authentic course concepts rather than an overstated corporate agency.

---

## 6. Final Objective
To create an authentic, professional, and visually engaging personal website that acts as a living demonstration of **Evidence-Driven Iterative AI Prompting**—proving that high-context prompting, human review, and real evidence yield an undeniably superior result compared to generic first attempts.
